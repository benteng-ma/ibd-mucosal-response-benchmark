# Phase 1 evidence ceiling

## Highest justified conclusion

Published mucosal IBD treatment-response programs show **heterogeneous, context-dependent external transportability**. Two of three strict external cells preserved directional separation with bootstrap intervals above chance, but no strict cell survived Holm correction. Most cross-therapy cells were uncertain.

## What may be claimed

- Study-specific discrimination of frozen directional program scores in named public tasks.
- Directional support where the full bootstrap interval lies above 0.50.
- Failure or uncertainty of transport where the interval includes 0.50.
- Descriptive differences between strict, endpoint/disease-transport and cross-therapy stress-test strata.
- Absence of data-lineage leakage under the frozen rules.

## What may not be claimed

- No program is multiplicity-controlled “validated” by the strict family.
- No program is a universal or pan-biologic response biomarker.
- No AUROC difference between programs is formally established.
- No calibrated probability, threshold, clinical utility or treatment-selection benefit is available.
- Cross-therapy association does not imply a shared causal resistance mechanism.
- Unexecuted E-MTAB-7845 cells cannot be called negative.
- Repeated UNIFI endpoints and multiple program cells cannot be counted as independent replications.

## Manuscript ceiling

A manuscript may report a lineage-aware external benchmark whose central result is limited and heterogeneous transportability, including null findings. It should be framed as retrospective analytical validation of program direction under explicit contexts, not prospective clinical validation.
